What we do

You pick the site, the days, and the hours. We send the officers.

Security guards

Officers at doors, gates, and front desks. They check people in and keep your site under control.

Event security

Entry checks, bag checks, and crowd management for concerts, festivals, weddings, and private events.

Mobile patrol

Officers drive to your property on a schedule, check doors, gates, and lots, and report what they find.

Fire watch

Officers walk your building when fire alarms or sprinklers are down, and log every round.
